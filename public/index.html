The pitch
behind crumbz.

01

The bin is winning

A

Retail throws out what it can't sell in time

Supermarkets, grocery stores and vendors discard millions of tonnes of packed food yearly for approaching expiry — not because it's unsafe.

B

No route from surplus shelf to hungry buyer

There's no scalable platform connecting near-expiry surplus to buyers at a discount, or to donation, before it's tossed.

C

Warehouses waste while people go without

Food piles up while millions remain food insecure — a gap that persists for lack of last-mile delivery and listing infrastructure.

D

Every discarded item wastes what made it

Discarded food drives landfill methane and wastes the energy, water and resources spent producing and packaging it.

02

How crumbz works

01

List it

Vendors, supermarkets or individuals list near-expiry packed items — item, quantity, expiry date, and price or free donation.

02

Price it, smartly

The pricing model sets a discount based on days to expiry, live in the listing form — the closer to expiry, the steeper the cut.

03

Route it through an outlet

Listings flow to a Crumbz city outlet acting as an aggregation and quality-check hub.

04

Browse and claim

Buyers filter by category, expiry window and outlet, then choose pickup or delivery at checkout.

05

Deliver it, fast

Delivery partners fulfil home orders within each outlet's service radius.

06

Or donate it

Items listed as donations go free to buyers, or route to registered NGOs.

03

Weighing it honestly

+ Advantages
  • Direct SDG 2 impact — less food wasted, more food reaching price-sensitive buyers and donation recipients.
  • Open listing model — anyone can list, maximising recovery volume.
  • Dual access modes — pickup and delivery lower the barrier for everyone.
  • Scalable outlet network — expands city by city at low overhead.
− Disadvantages
  • Quality perception risk — some buyers may distrust near-expiry items regardless of intact packaging.
  • Logistics complexity — coordinating listers, outlets and delivery needs robust real-time systems.
  • Short listing lifecycle — tight availability windows demand real-time inventory management.
